# M9 — UI integration and feature completion: acceptance report

**Status: every M9 acceptance criterion passes in CI on four Android versions (API 29, 34, 35 and 36), in all three attempts of one CI run of the same commit, after two independent reviews of the work, seven rounds of review of their fixes and a fact-check of this report; every finding was fixed or, where the behaviour is a deliberate trade-off, documented. One condition from M1 is still open: the GO recommendation asked for the lab checks E01 and E07–E10 to be reproduced on a physical phone with packet capture before UI integration work is called done (`docs/m1/09-feasibility-report.md` §7, condition 1), and every run so far is on emulators — that check is M10's, on a phone this workspace doesn't have. Awaiting the product owner's review of pull request #8. Pull requests #5–#7 were merged on 4 November, #6 and #7 into their stacked base branches rather than `main`, so `main` holds M6 only; #8 now targets `main`, and merging it brings M7, M8 and M9 there. ADR-0014 was accepted under the owner's delegation and can be reopened. Decisions D2, D6, D7 and D8 stay with the owner, and D9 is new.**

## Reviews and what they changed

Two independent reviews read the work, each told to find defects rather than confirm them: the session, the tester, the tile and the form's logic (`b7204fd..ac971de`), and the screens, the copy, the documents and the scripts (`9e4402f`). Each of the seven rounds of fixes that followed was reviewed in turn by a fresh agent; the last, `cef3b29`, takes the sixth review's own test, and that reviewer checked it and found it clean. Two commits between rounds were not reviewed on their own: `af266eb` (the profile card's ACTIVE label wrapping under a long name, found reviewing the 200 % images — a layout change and its images; the next review read the file it changed) and `f574753` (two images); their images were looked at before they were committed. Three findings were rated high: the generated notices and 19 reference screenshots not committed, and the touch-target check measuring the wrong bounds (review B), and the notices still missing after the first fixes (review D); the rest were medium or below. Every finding was fixed or, where noted, documented.
