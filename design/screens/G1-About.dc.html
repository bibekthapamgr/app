About & privacy

ProxyFlow Version 1.0.0 (100) · free, no account

PRIVACY

  • No account, ads or analytics. ProxyFlow has no servers of its own.
  • Profiles, encrypted passwords and logs stay on this phone.
  • Your proxy provider can see which sites you visit and any unencrypted data.

WHAT IT CAN CARRY

  • Websites and most apps (TCP) and website names (DNS) go through your proxy.
  • UDP (calls, some games), ping and local devices like printers are blocked.
  • IPv6 addresses work only if your proxy supports IPv6.
