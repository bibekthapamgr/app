While connected

Websites and most apps (TCP) Via your proxy
Website names (DNS) Resolved by proxy
Calls, some games, ping (UDP, ICMP) Blocked
Printers, casting, local devices Not reachable

Blocked traffic is never sent around your proxy. Video calls usually switch to TCP; apps that can't will not work while connected.

Your proxy provider can see which sites you connect to and any unencrypted data. Choose one you trust.
